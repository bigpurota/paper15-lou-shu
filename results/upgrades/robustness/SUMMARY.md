## Caveats

- The block bootstrap with block 6 keeps autocorrelation only within a block; the percentile CI with 87–93 months is approximate; the non-circular MBB underweights the ends of the sample.
- 'borderline' in the bootstrap table: the CI bound is so close to 0 that under other seeds (range of the lower bound in `bootstrap_ci.csv`, columns ci95_lo_seed_*) the CI sometimes excludes and sometimes includes 0; we do not count such a result as significant.
- The world-0 thresholds are estimated from 200 replications; the standard error of the false-positive rate is ≈1.9 pp, and the 95% quantile of |t| is noisy too.
- The paper's effect per 1 s.d. is taken from its text (s.d. 2.53); the paper does not say whether this is a pooled or an average cross-sectional s.d.; ours is the average cross-sectional s.d. The ratio of the MDE to the paper's effect is an estimate.
- Dropping a year joins the neighbouring months in the NW estimate; betas/CMOM are not re-estimated.
- The simulated MDE depends on the DGP (noise, number of coins); rescaling to the real N×T is an estimate, not a measurement.
- The HLZ bar t>3 is shown next to 1.96; in world 0 NW(6) over-rejects, so 1.96 is laxer than its nominal level.
