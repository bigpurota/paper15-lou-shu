## Interpretation (of the numbers above)
Thresholds: |t| > 1.96 (5%) and t > 3.0 (Harvey–Liu–Zhu, 2016).

- Volume component in FM (dependent variable: the return adjusted for its beta to the EW TQBR market; the paper uses FF3-adjusted returns): `ln A_C` in model (4): 0.298 (t = 2.85): significant (|t| > 1.96), same sign as the paper; `ln AT_C`: 0.310 (t = 2.87): significant (|t| > 1.96), same sign as the paper; model (1) `ln A`: 0.313 (t = 3.01): significant (|t| > 1.96), clears the HLZ bar t > 3.0, same sign as the paper.
- Price-impact component: `Res.lnA` in model (4): -0.038 (t = -0.13): not significant, same sign as the paper; `Res.lnAT`: -0.216 (t = -0.66): not significant, same sign as the paper; none is significantly positive, so this part of the paper's conclusion ('price impact is not priced') is reproduced only in its weak form (no positive price-impact premium); the paper's significantly negative `Res.lnA` (-0.248, t = -3.14) is not reproduced (-0.038, t = -0.13).
- Sorts (raw returns): H–L on `A`: 1.306 (t = 1.81): not significant, same sign as the paper; on `A_C`: 1.108 (t = 1.53): not significant, same sign as the paper; on `Res.A`: 0.462 (t = 1.23): not significant, sign opposite to the paper.
- H–L alphas on MKT+SMB+MOM: `A`: 0.052 (t = 0.18): not significant, same sign as the paper; `A_C`: 0.034 (t = 0.13): not significant, same sign as the paper. The alpha uses 138 months (all three factors are needed), the raw H–L uses 145; over the same 138 months the raw H–L on `A_C` is 1.331 (t = 1.90) and on `A` 1.497 (t = 2.09). Loadings of the `A_C` H–L (`T3_alpha_detail.csv`): SMB 0.91 (t = 8.54), MOM 0.39 (t = 3.52), MKT -0.14 (t = -3.10); mean ln ME of the bottom/top quintile: 13.22 / 8.49. The raw spread (over the same months) is almost entirely absorbed by the factors: the alpha is less than a third of the raw H–L, and the main loading is on SMB (the illiquid quintile consists of small stocks).
- Sensitivity to the risk adjustment: FM (4) `ln A_C` on raw returns: 0.100 (t = 1.15), `ln A` in model (1): 0.122 (t = 1.43); on raw returns over the same months as the main FM (post hoc diagnostic): 0.167 (t = 1.97) and 0.184 (t = 2.21).
- Concentration by year (post hoc, `diag_by_year.csv`, each year dropped in turn): the largest contribution to the cumulative H–L on `A_C` comes from 2023, 65% (on average 8.69%/month over 12 months); without it H–L = 0.424 (t = 0.97), and FM (4) `ln A_C` = 0.226 (t = 2.79); the minimum t of FM (4) `ln A_C` when any one year is dropped: 2.34.
- Summary: the volume component is significant in the main FM in all models of both families (|t| > 1.96); only these clear the HLZ bar t > 3.0: FM (1) lnA (t = 3.01), FM (5) lnA_C (t = 3.19), FM (5) lnAT_C (t = 3.04) (none in model (4)); on raw returns it is not significant, in the `A_C` sorts it is not significant, in the portfolio alphas it is not significant. The direction (volume, not price impact) matches the paper, but the evidence on MOEX is weaker and depends on the specification; see all variants below.

## Caveats
- `ISSUESIZE` is current only (ISS does not provide its history): past turnover and `ME` use today's share count (volume is converted to today's units using splits).
- Dividends: the ISS endpoint `/securities/<SECID>/dividends` is retired, so dividends come from dohod.ru (fallback smart-lab.ru): pages exist for 292 of 356 stocks, 1476 payments. Check of record dates: slope of the ex-date price drop on the dividend yield 0.66 (day before: 0.01); source cross-check: of 909 payments found on both dohod.ru and smart-lab.ru, the ex-dates agree in 96% of cases (within ±1 trading day: 98%); 275 of 1184 dohod.ru payments are not found on smart-lab.ru with the same amount (±2%) and date (±5 days): smart-lab has a shorter history and rescales amounts differently after splits.
- Trading halt 2022-02-28..2022-03-23: no daily return is computed across the gap; the February→March 2022 monthly return is kept (it is the holder's real return).
- No B/M; the risk adjustment is the EW TQBR market (betas over 60 months, minimum 24), alphas on MKT+SMB+MOM, r_f = 0.
- 116–208 stocks per month (measure months): power is lower than in the paper (588 months, 1,775 stocks on average); quintiles hold 23–41 stocks, so terciles are reported as robustness.
- Tickers that changed SECID (e.g. TCSG→T) are treated as different stocks; returns are not spliced at the switch.
- Data corrections after the first run (M9; checked manually against public sources, which are described in `MOEX_SPEC` and the run manifest, `spec.data`; no links or copies of the sources are stored in the repository): split BELU 1:8 2024-08-22 (not in the ISS split history); dividend LNZL (registry 2021-07-13, yield 65%, above the 50% error guard). Flags are in `data_flags.csv`; the variant without the corrections is in the table above.
- The scale of `ivol` (ours: s.d. of daily residuals, in fractions) may differ from the paper's, so for `ivol` we compare sign and t, not magnitude.
- Full list of deviations: `deviations.csv` (M1–M9).
