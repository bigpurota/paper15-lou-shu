**Match rule.** Coefficients and spreads: if the paper is significant (|t| ≥ 1.96), *yes* = same sign and |t| ≥ 1.96, *sign only* = same sign but not significant, *no* = opposite sign. If the paper is not significant, *yes* = we are not significant either, *sign only* / *no* = we are significant with the same / opposite sign. Levels (correlations, Q75/Q25): *yes* = same sign and within half an order of magnitude (|log10(us/paper)| ≤ 0.5), *sign only* = same sign otherwise; Q75/Q25 ratios are positive by construction, so they get *yes* or *no* on magnitude alone. *Months needed* (in the A_C sort row) assumes the SE falls as 1/sqrt(T): an estimate. Units: H-L and alphas in %/month; FM slopes in %/month per unit of the regressor (paper: FF3-adjusted; us: CMKT-adjusted, D4). Deviations D1–D18, upgrade deviations U1–U8 and journal entries J1–J2: `DEVIATIONS.md`.

## Size and power of the tests (known-answer simulations, `results/simulations/`)

- **World 0, no premium** (200 runs, 3.49% mean daily |r| vs 4.13% in our data): with NW(6) the share of runs with |t| > 1.96 is 8.0% for FM ln A_C, 9.5% for FM Res. ln A (95% CI 5.8–14.4) and 6.5% for H-L A_C. Without the NW correction (lag 0), Res. ln A gives 5.0%. Empirical 5% critical |t|: FM ln A_C 2.21, FM Res. ln A 2.15, H-L A_C 2.10, H-L A 2.00. With about 90 months NW(6) over-rejects for Res. ln A (the 95% CI excludes 5%). The share with |t| > 3 is 1.5% (Res. ln A).
- **World V, power** (20 runs per level; t > 1.96, FM ln A_C / H-L A_C) at a premium of 0.25, 0.5 and 1%/mo per s.d. of the priced state: 35/45%, 95/85%, 100/100%. These overstate power on real data. The sampling s.d. of the H-L A_C spread across world-0 runs is 0.45%/mo, while our real NW s.e. is 1.52%/mo (3.4x). Real returns are noisier and have fat tails (s.d. 43.9%/mo, p99 158.4%, max 1149.7%). The MDE80 column therefore uses the real standard errors. Fuller analysis (block bootstrap, leave-one-year-out, multiple testing): `results/upgrades/robustness/`.
- **Placebos** (`placebo.csv`): shuffling signals within month, at most 10% of 20 runs give |t| > 1.96 for any statistic. Using the month-t signal instead (lag 0, a deliberate leak) gives t -6.02 for H-L A_C and -4.85 for FM ln A_C, so a timing error in the signal lag would visibly change the results. January-only ln A_C slope: 0.70 (t 0.39).
