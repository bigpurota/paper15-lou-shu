\speaker{Marat Bineman}
\begin{frame}{Problem: the Amihud measure is priced, but for what?}
\msg{Stocks that are illiquid by the Amihud measure earn a premium. It is usually read as compensation for price impact. Lou \& Shu ask whether that reading is right.}
\[ A_{i,t} = \frac{1}{D}\sum_{d} \frac{|r_{i,d}|}{\mathrm{DVol}_{i,d}} \]
\begin{itemize}
\item The paper's premium: quintile H-L on $A$, \V{pvu.HLArawCoef.paper} \%/month (NYSE/AMEX 1964–2012, \V{d.paperMonths} months).
\item If the premium is for price impact, $A$ serves both to \emph{measure} liquidity and to \emph{explain} returns. The paper argues that these are two different questions.
\end{itemize}
\bigskip
{\small\color{muted}Literature: the measure (Amihud 2002) → validation against high-frequency benchmarks (Goyenko–Holden–Trzcinka 2009) → what is priced (Lou \& Shu 2017) → critique of the decomposition (Amihud \& Noh 2021) → spread from OHLC (EDGE, Ardia–Guidotti–Kroencke 2024).}
\end{frame}

\speaker{Sofya Shapovalova}
\begin{frame}{Method: a ``constant'' measure and a residual}
\msg{If $A_C$ is priced and the part of $A$ beyond $A_C$ is not, the Amihud premium is a volume premium.}
\[ A_C = \frac{1}{D}\sum_d \frac{1}{\mathrm{DVol}_d} \]
Res.$\ln A$: the residual of a cross-sectional regression of $\ln A$ on $\ln A_C$, estimated each month.
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.48\textwidth}
\textbf{``Volume'' hypothesis}\par $A_C$ is priced, the residual is not
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.48\textwidth}
\textbf{``Price impact'' hypothesis}\par the residual Res.$A$ is priced
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\medskip
\textbf{First we tested on simulations whether the pipeline tells the hypotheses apart} (\V{sim.assets} coins × \V{sim.months} months; \V{sim.checks.pass} of \V{sim.checks.n} criteria pass, the world-0 size criterion fails):
\begin{itemize}
\item world V (volume premium): $A_C$ significant in \V{sim.V.fm2} of runs, Res.lnA in \V{sim.V.fm3};
\item world P (price-impact premium): Res.lnA significant in FM in \V{sim.P.fm3}, in the quintile sort only in \V{sim.P.hlResA}; but $\ln A_C$ is also falsely significant in \V{sim.P.fm2}.
\item Simulated noise is smaller than real noise: the real SE of the $A_C$ spread is \V{d.seRatioHlAC} times the simulated one, so power on real data is much lower.
\end{itemize}
\src{results/simulations/sim\_world\_summary.csv; results/upgrades/robustness/}
\end{frame}

\speaker{Alexia Fox}
\begin{frame}{Critique}
\msg{The paper's weak link is the interpretation of the residual; ours is power and one month.}
\begin{columns}[c]
\begin{column}{0.42\textwidth}
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{\basedir/fig_hl_cumulative.png}
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.56\textwidth}
\scriptsize
\begin{enumerate}
\item The decomposition drops the covariance term (A\&N). In our data the residual is mostly $\ln|Ret|$ (R² \V{an.r2_res_abs}); by construction it is almost entirely $\ln|Ret|$ + DIF (R² \V{an.r2_res_dif}). The paper controls IVOL (Table 4, model 5), and IVOL alone does not absorb it for us either (\V{an.V3m}); the IVOL-puzzle link is a hypothesis.
\item ``Volume, not liquidity'' may be a false dichotomy: liquid assets trade more (Amihud–Mendelson 1986).
\item Hypothesis, untested: Lee–Ready errors could attenuate the paper's λ (the paper checks its λ against Hasbrouck's estimates). Our λ is priced negatively, so ``a noisy λ hides a premium'' is not supported by our data.
\item US before 2012 only: after 2012 the raw OpenAP Illiquidity L$-$S fell (\V{op.ill.pre} → \V{op.ill.post}); its FF3+UMD alpha is insignificant in both periods (\V{op.pre.ff4} → \V{op.post.ff4}) and the SMB loading fell (\V{op.pre.smb} → \V{op.post.smb}): consistent with lower size exposure, not tested formally. OpenAP (NYSE only, price above 5 dollars, annual rebalancing) is not the paper's design (NYSE/AMEX, monthly $A$ at $t-2$, monthly rebalancing).
\end{enumerate}
Our side: without 2021 the $A_C$ spread is \V{x21.hlAC}. On MOEX the positive $\ln A_C$ slope repeats at 1.96 (model (2) \V{mx.fm2}, not HLZ; raw returns: model (2) \V{mx.raw.fm2ac}, model (4) \V{mx.raw.fm4ac}), but the paper's negative Res.lnA does not (\V{mx.fm4res}).
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

\speaker{Alexia Fox}
\begin{frame}{Overfitting: could these results be overfitted?}
\footnotesize
\textbf{Authors:} subperiods, NASDAQ out of sample, annual measures, raw returns.\par\smallskip
\textbf{Us:}
\begin{itemize}
\item Base specification committed before the first run (git \V{git.prereg}); later changes J1, J2 logged. In that first run no T4 slope reached 1.96 (Res.lnA (3) \V{fr.fm3res}); the J1 audit rules were designed after it, so the Res.lnA significance depends on a post-first-run data fix. Upgrade specifications and verdict rules were not pre-registered in git; post hoc variants are labelled.
\item Ledger of every t: base family \V{lg.base.n} tests, |t| > 1.96 for \V{lg.base.196} (\V{lg.base.exp}–\V{w0.exp_base} expected under the null; most are correlated variants of the Res.lnA hypothesis, so this is not a formal test); |t| > 3 for \V{lg.base.3}; Bonferroni/BH \V{lg.base.bonf}/\V{lg.base.bh}.
\item Size in world 0: NW(6) gives \V{w0.fpr_min}–\V{w0.fpr_max} false |t| > 1.96; empirical 5\% cut-off \V{w0.crit_min}–\V{w0.crit_max}. Simulations: \V{sim.checks.pass} of \V{sim.checks.n} criteria pass (\V{sim.checks.loose_n} under a loose ``share \V{sim.checks.loose_rule}'' rule; \V{sim.checks.pass_strict5} with 5\%); failed: {\color{warn}\V{sim.checks.failed}}.
\item Month-block bootstrap: a CI excluding zero is stable for only \V{rb.robust} of \V{rb.n} statistics (both Res.lnA). Placebo: shuffling the measures within month (\V{plc.shuffle_reps} permutations) gives |mean t| $\le$ \V{plc.shuffle_maxabs_meant}, s.d. of t \V{plc.shuffle_sd_t_min}–\V{plc.shuffle_sd_t_max}, share |t| > 1.96 $\le$ \V{plc.shuffle_share196_max}; a lag-0 signal (contemporaneous, not a pure leak test) gives |t| up to \V{plc.leak_maxabs_t}.
\item Survivorship: later-delisted pairs kept (count not recorded), no delisting returns. Costs: break-even \V{ed.breakeven} per side, from an insignificant gross spread. Non-stationarity: $\ln A_C$ \V{pvu.SubAlnAC} in the first half, \V{pvu.SubBlnAC} in the second.
\item Out of sample: MOEX (variants not pre-registered in git), US after 2012 (ready-made OpenAP portfolios). Robustness, not out of sample: without 2021.
\end{itemize}
