\speaker{Sofia Shapovalova}
\begin{frame}{Method: a ``constant'' measure and a residual}
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.44\textwidth}
\lead{If $A_C$ is priced and the part of $A$ beyond $A_C$ is not, the Amihud premium is a volume premium.}
\[ A_C = \frac{1}{D}\sum_d \frac{1}{\mathrm{DVol}_d} \]
\[ \ln A = a_t + b_t \ln A_C + \mathrm{Res.}\ln A \]
\smalltxt
First we tested on simulations whether the pipeline tells the hypotheses apart (\V{sim.assets} coins × \V{sim.months} months; \V{sim.checks.nfail} of \V{sim.checks.n} criteria fail):
\begin{itemize}
\item world V: $A_C$ significant in \V{sim.V.fm2} of runs, Res.lnA in \V{sim.V.fm3};

\begin{frame}{B1. Definitions}
\fontsize{7.5}{9.5}\selectfont
\begin{itemize}
\item $A = \mathrm{mean}_d(|r_d|/\mathrm{DVol}_d)$, $A_C = \mathrm{mean}_d(1/\mathrm{DVol}_d)$; Res.lnA is the residual of $\ln A$ on $\ln A_C$, estimated each month.
\item Res.$\ln A$ regression: $\ln A = a_t + b_t \ln A_C + \mathrm{Res.}\ln A$.
\item Amihud \& Noh: $\ln A = \ln|Ret| + \ln A_C + \mathrm{DIF}$; DIF reflects $\mathrm{cov}(|r|, 1/\mathrm{DVol})$.
\item λ (Hasbrouck): 5-minute return on $\sum \mathrm{sign}\cdot\sqrt{\mathrm{DVol}}$; ours uses SVol $= \sqrt{B} - \sqrt{S}$ from taker volume per bar.
\item IMLA$_C$: tercile H-L on $A_C$; CMKT: EW market of the universe; CMOM: tercile H-L on Ret[$-12$,$-2$].
\end{itemize}

\begin{frame}{B7. What was not done and limitations}
\fontsize{6.4}{8}\selectfont
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Reproducibility:} no single clean-clone run both redid the downloads and ran \texttt{make all} (\texttt{make all} of the final code used cached data; the from-scratch clone ran an earlier version of the code, and its hf\_lambda step needed \V{repro.hf_n} attempts); macOS only.
\item \textbf{Simulations:} the world-0 size criterion fails for Res.lnA (\V{sim.0.fm3}); \V{sim.checks.loose_n} passes use a loose ``share \V{sim.checks.loose_rule}'' rule.
\item \textbf{Pre-registration:} the Res.lnA significance appears only after the J1 audit, designed after the first run; verdict rules and upgrade specifications were not pre-registered; post hoc diagnostics (λ checks, MOEX same-months check, without the most influential month) are labelled.
\item \textbf{Unevidenced checks:} no stored announcement sources, no saved cache comparison; the number of delisted pairs is not recorded; no delisting returns; the paper's numbers were checked against the PDF by hand, with no automated test.
\item \textbf{Not done:} market caps (size control, VW by cap); the t+1 leak placebo; corr($AT_C$, λ); λ from midquotes.
\item \textbf{Formalities:} the slides were due 24 hours before the defence (16 October, 13:00), but the deck was first saved on \V{deck.first}: deadline missed. Latin spelling of the names not confirmed by the members; no timed rehearsal recorded.
\end{itemize}
